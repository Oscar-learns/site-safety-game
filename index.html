Site Safety Inspection

Your group walks the site in AR and finds the hazards. Same site for every group.

1. Choose your team
  1. Tap Start. The site opens as a 3D model: drag to rotate, pinch to zoom, tap a hazard to pick its grid cell.
  2. Tap View on my table (AR) to place the site on your table with your phone. Place it once, then walk around and look inside. Tap nothing while you look.
  3. Come back here and open Notes to log each hazard: grid cell, type, short description. Use the map to find the cell.
