Site Safety Inspection

Your group walks the site in AR and finds the hazards. Same site for every group.

1. Choose your team
  1. Tap Start AR and allow the camera and motion access.
  2. Aim your phone at the spot on your table where the site should stand. Adjust the size, then tap Place here. It stays fixed there.
  3. Slide a finger to rotate the site. Turn your phone to look around it. Tap a hazard to flag its grid cell.
  4. Open Notes to log each hazard: grid cell, type, short description.
